## 中文生圖 Prompt

一張紀實攝影風格的老照片，背景是昏黃的窗光，一位身穿白袍的中年男性醫生坐在窗邊，手中握著一張泛黄的照片，眼神深邃而堅毅。畫面溫暖但克制，展示醫者的內心掙扎與堅毅。

## English Image Prompt（備用）

A realistic photography style old photograph, with a dim window light background. An elderly male doctor in white coat is sitting by the window, holding a yellowed photo in his hand, with a profound and resolute gaze. The atmosphere is warm but restrained, showcasing the inner struggle and resilience of doctors.
